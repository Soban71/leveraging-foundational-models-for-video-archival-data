Replay is a Belfast based children's data company. We make theatre for all ages from 0 to 19 and we also have a programme of work for children and young people who have profound and multiple learning difficulties. We would always take work to the Belfast Children's Festival and we're part of a loose group called Theatre V Young Audiences Northern.
