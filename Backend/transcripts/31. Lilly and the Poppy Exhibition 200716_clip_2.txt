And of course, we here in Northern Ireland identify very much with the 36th Ulster Division formed in Ulster at the start of the First World War. And many hundreds of orange men were within the ranks of the division as they charged over the top that day. So this year the Lillian and Poppy exhibition, the Lillian obviously for the orange institution and the Poppy which is the International Symbol of Remembrance for the Royal British Legion, we thought we would combine the two as part of our motif for commemorating and remembering those members of the institution who sacrificed so much.
