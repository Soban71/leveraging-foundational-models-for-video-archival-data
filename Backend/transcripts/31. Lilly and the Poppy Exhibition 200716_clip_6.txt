Indeed, one particular orange man when the advance was faltering at one stage, he lifted his sash above his head and shouted, come on, boys, no surrender. That rallied the troops. It was and was remarked at the time by the commanding officer of the Elstred Division, that by a quirk of fate, I suppose, the 1st of July was the anniversary under the old calendar of the Battle of the Boyne in 1690.
